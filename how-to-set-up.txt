CLINIC EXPENSES - SET-UP GUIDE (for Dr. Owais)
================================================
Menu names on Google, Netlify and Apple screens change from time to time, so read the steps as a guide, not a script.
If a screen looks different, tell me what you see and I will adjust the steps.

PART 1 - Google Sheet that receives the pharmacist's entries (about 5 minutes, on your computer or phone browser)
1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet. Name it "Clinic Expenses".
2. Menu: Extensions > Apps Script. Delete everything in the editor.
3. Open the file apps-script.gs (in this folder), copy ALL of it, and paste it into the editor.
4. On the line   const KEY = "CHANGE-THIS-SECRET-WORD";   replace the words inside the quotes with your own secret word
   (letters and numbers, no spaces, e.g. clinic7731). Write it down. Click the Save icon.
5. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon and choose "Web app".
     Execute as: Me
     Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy. Google will ask you to authorise. Choose your account. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app",
   click Advanced > Go to (project name) > Allow. This is your own script on your own sheet.
6. Copy the "Web app URL" it shows (it ends with /exec). That is the "Sheet link".
   It must look like https://script.google.com/macros/s/..../exec . A link containing "/macros/library/" is the wrong one
   (that comes from Deploy > New deployment > Library) and will not work.
   To build the link into the app so the phone fills it in by itself, open index.html, find the line
   const SHEET_URL="";   and put the /exec link inside the quotes, then upload the folder to Netlify again.
7. Optional: in the editor choose the function setupSummary at the top and click Run once. It adds a "Summary" tab.
   (This one tab uses a Google Sheets formula that I could not test here. The "Expenses" tab is the real record.)
IMPORTANT: if you ever edit the script later, use Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy.
Otherwise the old version keeps running.

PART 2 - Put the app on the internet once, so the iPhone can install it
The app must be reachable at a secure (https) address once. After the pharmacist installs it, it works with no internet.
Easiest free way (Netlify Drop):
1. On your computer, open app.netlify.com/drop in a browser.
2. Drag the whole "finance-helper" FOLDER (unzipped) onto the page.
3. It gives you an address like https://something.netlify.app . Sites made without an account may be deleted after a short time,
   so create the free Netlify account when it offers to keep the site. Open the address on your own phone to confirm it loads.
(GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages also work, but need more steps.)

PART 3 - Install on the pharmacist's iPhone
1. On the iPhone, open Safari (it must be Safari) and go to the address from Part 2.
2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
3. Open the new "Expenses" icon from the home screen. Open it once while online so it saves itself.
4. Settings (bottom right):
     Sheet link = the /exec address from Part 1
     Key        = your secret word
     This phone's name = e.g. Pharmacist iPhone
   Tap "Save and test". You should see "Connected".
5. Optional: set a PIN in Settings.

DAILY USE FOR THE PHARMACIST
- Tap Add, type the amount, choose what it is for (salary, fuel, clinic expense, restocking, personal), choose the category, name, Save.
- Works with no internet. Entries show an hourglass until they reach your sheet, then a tick.
- Tap an entry in "Entries" to edit or delete it.

WHAT YOU SEE
- Open your "Clinic Expenses" Google Sheet: every entry is a row in the "Expenses" tab (deleted ones are marked "yes" in Deleted).
- The same entry can never appear twice, even if the phone sends it again.

THINGS TO KNOW
- Entries live on the phone until they are sent. If the phone is lost or reset before sending, unsent entries are lost.
  Settings > "Save a backup file" shares a copy (to Files, Google Drive, WhatsApp...). Doing this weekly is wise.
- If you forget the PIN, the only way in is to erase the app's data on that phone (sent entries stay safe in your sheet).
- Anyone who has the Sheet link AND the key could add rows to your sheet. Keep both private; to cut off access, change the KEY and re-deploy.
- This is separate from the main clinic program. The categories use the same names, so the two can be compared later.
